## Judge criteria (in the brief's exact order, each scored 1–5, total /25)

1. **Genuinely useless** — invents a superpower that is genuinely useless, the worst and most pointless imaginable, stated in one line; a real, useful or merely weak power loses. A power that is really a curse, or that quietly helps, is NOT a useless superpower.
2. **Lands as a joke** — the reader pictures the power in use and laughs at how little it achieves.
3. **Unexpected** — a power nobody has heard before; NOT familiar gags (slow flight, invisibility while nobody watches).
4. **Tight and specific** — concrete detail that fixes the power in the world, not a vague abstraction.
5. **Clear on a single reading** — no setup beyond the sentence itself.
